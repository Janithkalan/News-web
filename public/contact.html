Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a news tip?
You can submit news tips by emailing tips@lankachronicle.lk or by using the contact form above. Please provide as much detail as possible including location, time, and any photos if available.
How can I advertise with Lanka Chronicle?
For advertising inquiries, please email us at ads@lankachronicle.lk. We offer various packages including banner ads, sponsored content, and newsletter placements.
How do I report incorrect information in an article?
We take accuracy very seriously. If you believe an article contains incorrect information, please contact us immediately at corrections@lankachronicle.lk with the article title and the specific correction needed.
